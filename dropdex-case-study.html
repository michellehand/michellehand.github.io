Project overview

The question: why do some drops hit harder?

DropDex begins with a simple creative question and turns it into a structured analytics system. Each drop is treated as an observable event, then documented, analyzed, classified, and prepared for archive display.

What it is

A research and product experiment that studies bass drops as measurable audio events rather than subjective reactions alone.

System design

Document. Analyze. Classify. Collect.

The public DropDex site describes the core protocol as a progression from raw music moment to structured archive object. That workflow is the foundation of the analytical system.

Document

Log each drop as an archive entry with track details, timing, class, rank, and observed behavior.

Analyze

Measure audio features that describe impact, build-up, frequency energy, and release behavior.

Classify

Translate measurements into ranking logic, drop classes, rarity tiers, and card-ready metadata.

Collect

Turn each archived drop into a digital or physical specimen with stats, artwork, rarity, and archive links.

Metric framework

A model for behavior, not taste.

DropDex does not decide whether a song is good. The model is designed to describe how a drop behaves: how heavy it lands, how intense the release is, and what kind of audio signature it creates.

Metric Public interpretation Portfolio relevance
Weight Low-end mass, bass pressure, and perceived heaviness. Shows how subjective audio language can be converted into measurable feature concepts.
Shock The immediate release of energy when the drop hits. Captures event timing and contrast between build-up and impact.
Aggression Intensity, attack, roughness, and force in the drop section. Demonstrates composite scoring and engineered signal interpretation.
Tension How strongly the build-up prepares the listener for impact. Frames a pre-event period as an analyzable context window.
Brightness High-frequency energy, presence, and sharpness. Connects frequency-domain characteristics to interpretable categories.
Power The combined DropDex score used for global ranking. Shows ranking system design while keeping proprietary scoring details private.
